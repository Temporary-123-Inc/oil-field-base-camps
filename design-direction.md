# Design direction

**Subject:** A fast-response rental site for operations teams planning remote workforce housing and oil-field base camps.

**Direction:** Sunlit field-operations editorial with engineered red signals.

**Palette:** Rig Navy `#102638` for trust and structure; Signal Red `#e33a2f` for urgent actions; Sky Wash `#eaf3f7` for light operational panels; Warm White `#fbfaf7` for the primary canvas; Steel `#5d6b75` for utility copy.

**Type:** Arial Narrow/Arial display treatment with Inter/system body text, using a compact industrial headline rhythm and highly readable body scale.

**Layout:** Offset editorial hero → proof rail → asymmetric Super 9 grid → deployment steps → calculator → response CTA.

**Signature:** Red route-line markers and coordinate-style utility labels that echo a field deployment map.

**Omitted:** Dark, heavy oil-industry chrome; the interface stays light so urgent actions and equipment imagery carry the weight.

Distinctive characteristics: clipped operational labels, an asymmetric nine-card inventory field, and red route-line accents connecting planning decisions.
